Potions and scrolls are unidentified: their colours mean something different every game, and you learn what one does by trying it. Walk into a chest to open it (locked ones need a golden key or some bashing). Hidden spike traps and trap doors lurk in the floor; purple portals teleport you. You get hungry, so eat. Monsters asleep show a z; pigs are peaceful unless you attack them.

Press any key to close.
