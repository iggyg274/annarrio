DIAG DASH — An Ann Arbor Sprint · World 1-1

Move ← → or A D  ·  Hurry hold Shift/Z  ·  Jump Space / ↑ / X (hold longer = higher)  ·  Pause P  ·  Mute M  ·  Restart R

Stomp the campus rovers. Collect maize coins (the “M” ones). Grab an espresso for FURY: faster legs, brick smashing, and a double jump. Reach the flag at Elsey Street.

City scenery art from the Urban City Tileset (Basic 32×32) — © Dlou Saiyan, used with permission for this project. Per its license the tileset may be used and modified here but must not be resold, redistributed, or repackaged; credit to Dlou Saiyan (dlousaiyan.com) is required and appears in-game.

Gameplay tiles, characters, and music are generated procedurally at runtime (Web Audio API oscillators + envelopes; no audio files). Ann Arbor landmarks appear as deliberate low-fi text signage over generic buildings — parody flavour, not real logos or building reproductions. No Ann Arbor-specific artwork is included in either asset pack.

Built as a self-contained set of files: open index.html and play. Nothing is fetched over the network.
